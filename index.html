A place for every
part of your shop.

The details matter. Explore the tools behind the counter, beyond the shelves, and underneath every number.

Point of sale

Keep checkout moving with product search, barcode entry, clear basket totals, and flexible payments.

What’s included in Point of sale
  • Search by product name or barcode; sell by the configured unit.
  • Combine cash, configured non-cash methods, and customer credit on one receipt.
  • Record every payment tender separately, with totals checked to the cent.
  • Open receipt history and handle returns or cancellations according to permissions.
  • Online checkout checks available stock before completing a sale.

Products & inventory

Give every product a record, every movement a reason, and every purchase cost a place in your inventory.

What’s included in Products & inventory
  • Manage categories, barcodes, selling prices, units, and product availability.
  • Track stock through FIFO purchase layers and inventory movement history.
  • Count physical stock with an absolute quantity and a conflict check against the opening balance.
  • Review stocktakes by date, reason, user, direction, and product.
  • See low-stock products and keep product edits separate from stock adjustments.

Suppliers & purchasing

Keep supplier details, orders, deliveries, and purchase costs connected from the start.

What’s included in Suppliers & purchasing
  • Create and manage supplier records and purchase orders.
  • Receive goods against an order and add them to stock layers.
  • Track incoming items and inspect purchase documents.
  • Compare purchase prices and review purchasing reports.
  • Cancel eligible purchase documents within the reconciliation rules.

Customers & credit

Know who bought what, what they owe, and which payments brought their balance down.

What’s included in Customers & credit
  • Maintain customer records and view their purchase and ledger history.
  • Attach credit to an identified customer, including a credit portion of a split sale.
  • Record debt repayments and follow the remaining balance.
  • Settle outstanding debt first when goods from a credit sale are returned.
  • Use the desktop cashier’s locally synced customer records and repayment queue offline.

Money & accounts

Keep the drawer, non-cash accounts, and money movements understandable at the end of the day.

What’s included in Money & accounts
  • Track the till and configured payment accounts.
  • Record deposits, withdrawals, account transfers, and balance adjustments.
  • Keep sales tenders separate from later customer debt repayments.
  • Report actual money refunded separately from debt settled by a return.
  • Trace balances back to sales, repayments, refunds, and money movements.

Reports & analytics

Look past turnover to the costs, returns, products, and patterns that explain your shop’s performance.

What’s included in Reports & analytics
  • Review sales trends, receipt counts, top products, and low stock.
  • Separate turnover, returns, net revenue, cost of goods, and profit.
  • See write-off cost and profit after write-offs without changing gross profit’s meaning.
  • Review purchase reports, price changes, and reconciliation-period reports.
  • Use company-local dates and inspect historical periods.

Online store & Telegram

Turn selected products into a browsable storefront and bring incoming orders into your workspace.

What’s included in Online store & Telegram
  • Publish selected catalog products in the Telegram marketplace.
  • Let shoppers search products and filter by shop or category.
  • Provide product details, a basket, checkout, and order history.
  • Offer pickup and delivery when enabled; collect contact details, addresses, and notes.
  • Confirm, complete, or cancel orders with a reason. Online orders alone do not deduct stock or record a sale.

Offline desktop cashier

Keep trading through a connection drop, with local records and a visible path back to sync.

What’s included in Offline desktop cashier
  • Download the catalog after signing in and registering the device online.
  • Store sales and local sale history on the cashier computer.
  • Look up synced customers, view debts, and queue repayments offline.
  • Send pending records when connectivity returns, with duplicate protection.
  • Inspect sync states, retry failed records, and receive desktop update notifications.

Cash shifts & counts

Open with a real count, close with a real count, and understand the drawer in between.

What’s included in Cash shifts & counts
  • Open and close shifts with physically counted cash.
  • Read expected cash from the same till balance used by Finance.
  • Compare expected and counted cash and record the reconciliation adjustment.
  • Review shift payment totals and actual refunds.
  • Surface late arrivals so records outside the shift window stay explainable.

Write-offs & supplier returns

Record damaged, spoiled, or defective goods as documents with the costs they actually consumed.

What’s included in Write-offs & supplier returns
  • Create write-off documents with product lines and quantities.
  • Keep the reason separate from the disposition: disposal or return to supplier.
  • Use FIFO consumption and freeze the actual consumed costs on the document.
  • Review loss costs separately from sales turnover.
  • Record a supplier’s cash refund as a separate finance movement when money actually changes hands.

Companies, people & access

Put each business in its own workspace and give each teammate the access their job needs.

What’s included in Companies, people & access
  • Select a company after signing in and keep its business data isolated.
  • Manage company settings and member access.
  • Grant module permissions with user or manager access; administrators can manage the company.
  • Configure the storefront, delivery, pickup, and published products.
  • Keep the global owner panel separate from company business sessions.

Reconciliation & data confidence

Close a trading period with traceable records and checks that explain discrepancies.

What’s included in Reconciliation & data confidence
  • Declare a reconciliation cutoff after running consistency checks.
  • Protect earlier sales, purchase documents, and returns from ordinary edits.
  • Carry forward stock, FIFO layers, customer debts, money, and open purchase orders.
  • Compare derived totals with independent records, grouped by company.
  • Keep drift findings distinct from known facts such as late offline receipts.

What does Sellary include?

A browser workspace, a desktop cashier, and a Telegram storefront. Modules cover the register, sales, customers, inventory, purchasing, online orders, reports, finance, and company settings. AI access is a separate module.

Can I sell without internet?

Yes, with the desktop cashier after online registration and the first catalog download. It saves sales locally and syncs them when the connection returns. The browser register requires a connection to the server.

Is the online store a separate catalog?

You choose which products from your Sellary catalog appear in the Telegram storefront. Shoppers can search, add items to a basket, select pickup or delivery when enabled, and follow their orders. Orders must be sold through the register to affect stock and revenue.

Can customers pay in more than one way?

Yes. One sale can combine multiple tenders, including cash, configured non-cash payment methods, and one credit portion. Credit requires an identified customer. Each tender is recorded separately.

Can I manage more than one company?

Yes. Users choose a company after signing in. Company data is kept separate, and member permissions determine which modules they can use and manage.

Does an AI assistant control the business?

Access is permission-based. Reports and document histories are read-only tools. The supported purchase workflow uses a signed preview followed by a commit. Physical stock counts, sales, refunds, finance movements, reconciliations, and permission changes remain human actions in the app.

Can the full Sellary app run on GitHub Pages?

This landing page and the user guide can. The working Sellary system needs its backend and database; the browser workspace, desktop cashier, and Telegram storefront connect to that backend.
